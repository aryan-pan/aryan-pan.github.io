Scaling model fitting in PsyNeuLink

Our PsyNeuLink backend distributes parameter evaluations, participant fits, and training simulations across Dask workers. This browser demo illustrates individual fitting.

An educational DDM illustration, not a benchmark. The page uses 525 precomputed drift-diffusion models: drift v from 0 to 2 and symmetric boundaries ±a from 0.4 to 1.6, with noise scale 1, a 0.01-second time step, and 0.2 seconds of non-decision time. Each model uses 4,000 simulated trials. The participant's 200 trials are simulated separately.

Moving a slider selects a precomputed prediction. “Fit” evaluates the joint choice/response-time histogram log likelihood at every grid point; the brief animation shows that search, not measured computation time. The curve shows the marginal response-time histogram. Scores use smoothed histogram probabilities. The match meter is a relative visual guide, not a statistical probability. This page does not execute PsyNeuLink, Dask, or a neural likelihood. Generalization, hierarchical estimation and benchmark performance are discussed on the poster.

Created for Aryan Pandey's US-RSE poster on distributed and hierarchical parameter estimation in PsyNeuLink. No personal data are collected. After an initial online visit, supported browsers cache the demo for offline use.
